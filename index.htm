About Lewis School

Where Investors Are Educated

Lewis School represents the Investor pillar of Nolan University, complementing the Nolan Institute of Global Business & Technology.

Finance & Investment Focused

Every programme is built exclusively around finance, investment and capital — not general business education.

Capital-Allocation Thinking

Develop the analytical capability and financial judgment to allocate capital intelligently and defend your decisions.

Global Market Perspective

Programmes taught from a global markets perspective — relevant across Africa, emerging markets and beyond.

Graduate-Level Rigour

From Professional Certificate to full Master's, every programme is graduate-level and technically demanding.

Practitioner Faculty

Learn from faculty who have worked in investment banking, capital markets, asset management and private equity.

Recognised Credentials

Receive a credential awarded by Nolan University — from Professional Certificate through to Master's degree.
